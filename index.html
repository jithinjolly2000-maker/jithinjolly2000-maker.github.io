01 / Approach

Logic first. Then the build.

I don't come from a computer-science background. My strength is understanding a messy process deeply, designing the system that fixes it, and then using AI to build it. Every project follows the same four steps.

STEP 01

Map the process

Who does what, where the time goes, where errors creep in.

STEP 02

Design the logic

Rules, data flows, edge cases and what goes to a human for review.

STEP 03

Build with AI

Turn the design into a working tool quickly, then test it hard.

STEP 04

Measure & refine

Before/after numbers, accuracy checks, then iterate.

Build steady. Ship often. Keep your balance.— my whole philosophy, basically
